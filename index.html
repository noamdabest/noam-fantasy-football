Projection = 70% season PPG + 30% last-3 PPG, each first adjusted for the strength of the defenses that player already faced (season PPG alone where last-3 data wasn’t available). Each upcoming week is then adjusted for how many fantasy points that opponent allows to the position (capped at about ±12%), plus byes and injuries. Week 11 also reflects Vegas game lines. Ranges are rough 10th–90th percentile estimates and don’t include injury risk. Home/away is shown but not adjusted. In the matchup column, #1 is the defense that allows the most points to that position. Target share is season to date; QB rushing is per game over the last 3 games. Injuries included: Maye (out), Bowers (assumed out Weeks 11–13), Nacua (IR). Opponent data is missing for some NYG, NYJ and LV rows and treated as average; players tagged “est.” have estimated PPR. Team: enter your lineup spots, replacement level and roster (saved on this device only). Trade: with a roster, it compares your total lineup points before and after the trade, counting byes and free-agent replacement; without one it gives a rough guide. Trade v2 adds a plain-language “why”, playoff-week weighting, win probability from the projection ranges, roster-spot checks and an optional two-sided view with the other team’s roster. League-wide players are added from nflverse data (through Week 10). Players tagged est. have fewer than three games or have not played in the last two weeks (they may be injured or benched; check the injury report). Projections for added players are NOAM estimates built with the same method (70% season, 30% last three, adjusted for defenses faced) but they are not the original engine output; the original players are unchanged.
